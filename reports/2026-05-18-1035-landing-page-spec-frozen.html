Mental model

  • Dry-run as topology validator: the goal is to fire every loop edge once (parallel fan-out, serial dependency, marketer-only blackboard write, critic gate, two stage-reports), not to ship a polished page.
  • Audience choice as tone lock: hiring/portfolio → polished, design-thinking-forward; constrains designer (whitespace, type) and marketer (claim accuracy, no hype) jointly so their parallel outputs converge.
  • Fan-out boundary is the spec: designer and marketer can run in parallel only because spec fixes audience + CTA + host before they start; without that frozen contract their outputs silently diverge.
  • Builder serializes on creative outputs: index.html cannot be written until layout (designer) + copy (marketer) both land, so the loop bottleneck is the slower of the two — pick smaller scope first if budget tight.
  • Critic as the only authorized claim-checker: founder synthesis is fast but partial; Critic verifies the "polished hiring surface" claim against the actual artifact, not against the spec.
